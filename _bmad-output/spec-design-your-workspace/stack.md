# Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js.
- **Hosting:** Vercel free (Hobby) tier. No paid services, paid databases, or anything outside free-tier limits.
- **Preview rendering:** 2D layered images. Each selected item's image is stacked on a base scene; 3D is out of scope for now.
- **Assets:** product and scene images are the project's own asset files, served with the app.
